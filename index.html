FIELD MANUAL

Let the numbers guide you.

Explore every safe tile to complete the survey. Each number counts dangerous cores in neighboring tiles that share an edge or corner. Mark the tiles you suspect. Music starts after your first interaction; the top-right controls let you mute music and sound effects separately.

Faceted solids
Choose Faceted solid to explore a whole stepped 3D object. Every tile is an equal-sized square, and numbers sit flat on its face. Surface area controls the tile count; corner cuts change the silhouette without changing the area. Tiles sharing an edge or corner are neighbors, including across ridges and inside corners. Hover or focus a tile to see its full neighborhood. Rotate to reach the back; every side counts toward completion.
Explore / Mark
Click to explore. Right-click or press and hold to mark. On touch screens, you can also switch to Mark in the toolbar.
Open nearby tiles
When the nearby marks match a tile’s number, double-click it to explore the remaining neighbors. Incorrect marks can still trigger a core.
Look around
Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Use Top view to see the layout, or Reset to restore the starting view.
Keyboard controls
Arrow keys select a tile; Enter / Space explore; F marks; V toggles top view; R starts a new survey, with confirmation during an active round.
